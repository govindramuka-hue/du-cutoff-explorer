Note: This tool is made to help students get a rough idea of DU cutoffs. Please keep in mind:

  • The cutoffs shown may have errors due to data mapping.
  • I only had data for the 1st and mid-entry rounds, so I’ve taken the minimum of both as the cutoff. Where available, mid-entry marks were used because they might better represent later rounds.
  • In a few rare cases, mid-entry cutoffs are higher than the 1st round, possibly because students with higher scores applied in mid-entry rounds. These are exceptions, not the norm.
  • The tool does not specify total marks, which can be 750 or 1000, so please check the marking scheme before drawing conclusions.
